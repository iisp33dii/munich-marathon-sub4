🏋️ Runner-Specific Strength & Mobility Hub
Performed before/after your 3 weekly runs — protecting tendons & preventing "The Wall"
Stacked Protocol
Objective: Strengthen the eccentric stabilizers (hamstrings, gluteus medius, and soleus calf complex) that absorb 3x bodyweight per foot strike over 42,000 marathon steps.
1. Bulgarian Split Squats 3 x 8-10 / leg
Target: Quad & Glute Max Strength
• Rear foot elevated on bench/couch.
• Lower under control for 3 seconds.
• Cue: Drive through front heel to engage glute.
2. Single-Leg RDL 3 x 8 / leg
Target: Hamstrings & Hip Stability
• Stand on one leg with slight knee bend.
• Hinge hips backward.
• Cue: Keep hips square to floor.
3. Soleus Heel Drops 3 x 15 / leg
Target: Achilles Tendon & Soleus
• Stand on step with knee bent 20°.
• Lower heel over 4 seconds.
• Cue: Essential for preventing calf cramps.
4. Side Plank + Abduction 3 x 30s / side
Target: Gluteus Medius
• Raise top leg 30° with toes forward.
• Cue: Prevents hip drop and IT band friction.
5. Deadbug Core 3 x 12 / side
Target: Transverse Abdominis
• Extend opposite arm/leg while pressing lower back into floor.
• Cue: Maintains posture past 30 km.
Objective: Rotational core strength and explosive hip extension to maintain high running economy (160+ spm cadence).
